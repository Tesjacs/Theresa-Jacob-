How I handle customer support

Mock portfolio demonstrations built to show my approach and process — not claims of actual client projects.

"My order was supposed to arrive several days ago and I haven't received it yet."
Listen→ Verify→ Investigate→ Communicate→ Resolve→ Follow Up
Sample Response

"Thank you for reaching out, and I'm sorry for the delay. I've checked your order and can see it's currently with our delivery partner — I'll confirm the exact status and get back to you within the next few hours with an updated delivery estimate. I'll also flag this for priority handling so it reaches you as soon as possible."

"The item I received doesn't match what I ordered, and I'm quite frustrated about it."
Customer Concern→ Understand the Issue→ Check Information→ Provide Solution→ Confirm Resolution
Sample Response

"I completely understand the frustration, and I appreciate you bringing this to my attention. I've reviewed your order details and can confirm the mix-up on our end. I'll arrange a replacement of the correct item right away, and I'll follow up personally once it's on its way to make sure everything is resolved to your satisfaction."

"I've been thinking about getting home insurance, but I'm not sure what I actually need."
Outbound Call→ Understand Need→ Ask Relevant Questions→ Qualify Prospect→ Schedule Appointment→ Transfer to Advisor
Sample Response

"That's a great question, and I can help point you in the right direction. Could you tell me a little about your property — for example, whether you own or rent, and if you've had coverage before? Based on that, I can connect you with one of our licensed advisors who can walk you through the options that best fit your situation."

"I'm not sure which product would actually work best for me — there are so many options."
Ask Questions→ Understand Preferences→ Identify Needs→ Recommend Option→ Confirm Satisfaction
Sample Response

"Happy to help you narrow that down. Could you tell me a bit more about what you'll mainly be using it for, and anything specific you'd like it to do? Based on that, I'd recommend the option that best matches your day-to-day needs — and I'll check in afterward to make sure it's working well for you."
